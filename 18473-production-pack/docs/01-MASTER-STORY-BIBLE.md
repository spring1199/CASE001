# Case #001 — 18473 Master Story Bible

## One-sentence story
A woman who believes she is investigating a missing stranger slowly discovers that she once loved him anonymously, that he knew who she was, and that finding him now may expose him to the very surveillance system he disappeared to escape.

## Protagonist
**Maral Enkhtuvshin**, 25, international-relations graduate and junior specialist at Arvantai's foreign affairs ministry. She is close to her mother, less emotionally close to her father, and is an only child. She is thoughtful, diplomatic and instinctively sees multiple sides of a conflict.

Several months before the game she survived a vehicle accident during an emergency relocation connected indirectly to a hidden shelter network. Physical injury plus severe psychological trauma left a circumscribed autobiographical memory gap. She remembers herself, family, career and general life, but the period containing Winter 47, ticket 18473 and Tenuun is fragmented or inaccessible.

## Missing man
**Tenuun Batzorig**, 25, software engineer. Eldest child of a poor but loving family; younger sister Tuya and younger brother Bilguun. As a child he was funny, sociable and sang often. Poverty turned his youthful ambition toward wealth: he wanted to create a startup, become financially free and lift his family out of scarcity.

Life and his own choices made him quieter. His ultimate dream stopped being wealth and became a modest private life: a large wooden house outside the city, a spouse, children, a dog, greenhouse, fruit and vegetables, travel and a garden.

### Core flaw
Tenuun wants to protect people but repeatedly decides for them.

### Contradictions
- wants peace but cannot ignore injustice;
- protects people but removes their autonomy;
- believes in truth but survives through lies.

### Deepest fear
To remain alive while being unable to live with the person he loves.

## Mentor and company
**Orgil Sodnom**, founder of **Tenkhleg Data Infrastructure**, discovers Tenuun at university, hires him and materially changes his family's life. Orgil is mentor, benefactor and later moral adversary.

At 22, Tenuun is asked to perform an unauthorized access in order to understand a government architecture relevant to a massive city contract. He is told to inspect structure, not personal data. The opportunity could finance his future startup and family security. He crosses the line.

## GRAPH
A city identity and relationship-resolution layer. It links pseudonymous records across separate services and estimates whether records belong to the same person and whether people are related through repeated co-location, payments, transport, access events and other lawful data sources.

GRAPH is not intrinsically evil. It has saved lives: missing children, trafficking cases, fraud, emergencies.

The hidden danger is its relationship-discovery capability and re-identification layer. It can infer undocumented associations before a formal investigation exists.

Tenuun helped improve entity-resolution accuracy. His own code made the system more useful—and more capable of mapping people.

## Ticket 18473
At 24, Tenuun handles a ministry support ticket from an anonymous user, `USER_6F21`. She sees him as `ENG_04`. The technical conversation continues for 217 days and evolves into private conversation.

Tenuun first falls for Maral's mind: she sees both sides of difficult questions. A troubleshooting voice call exposes her laugh. Tenuun privately writes that when she laughs, for a few seconds the world feels all right.

He sees her face only much later.

## The accidental meeting
Before either knows the other's identity, they are stranded by winter weather at the same roadside café outside Arvantai. They talk as strangers for about forty minutes. Tenuun describes his future wooden house and greenhouse. Maral jokes that strawberries are difficult and he should plant raspberries. Neither exchanges a name.

This becomes their simplest and most precious shared memory.

## Winter 47
A 17-year-old daughter of a high-level foreign visitor disappears during extreme cold and has a condition requiring urgent medical help. Maral is involved in crisis coordination and knows GRAPH has an emergency relationship-search capability, but she lacks authorization.

Waiting risks the girl's life. Maral performs an unauthorized GRAPH query.

Forty-seven minutes later the girl is located alive.

However, the girl had voluntarily fled an abusive/controlling family environment and was being protected by an informal network that hides vulnerable people in temporary safe locations. GRAPH does not only reveal the girl; it maps parts of the protective network.

A life is saved. Other people lose invisibility.

## Tenuun discovers Maral
Tenuun investigates the unauthorized query and initially sees it as government misuse. To identify the operator, he himself violates a boundary and uses identity-resolution capabilities.

He discovers the operator is Maral.

He also realizes Maral is `USER_6F21` and the woman from the roadside café.

He now knows all three are the same person. Maral knows none of this.

Tenuun hides her attribution in the audit trail. His rationale is protection. It is also an invasion of her autonomy.

## Shelter network and Saruul
Tenuun tracks the fallout and meets **Saruul**, a woman associated with the informal shelter network. She initially despises him because his technology helped expose people. He admits his role and offers not an apology but repair.

Tenuun begins corrupting or obscuring vulnerable relationship edges. He refuses to destroy GRAPH because it genuinely saves lives.

## Decoy plan
Tenuun develops a third path: create misleading graph topology so vulnerable people appear to converge on him. He makes himself a decoy super-node. Attention and suspicion shift toward him.

This protects people but makes Tenuun the system's most interesting target.

## Maral is pushed away
As risks grow, Tenuun becomes cold in ticket 18473 and finally tells Maral to stop reopening it. Maral understands this as abandonment.

She sends a final voice message whose key idea is:
> Do not decide what is right for me. Even if the truth hurts me, that pain is mine.

Tenuun keeps the recording but does not answer.

## Protocol 12
Tenuun discovers that Winter 47 became internal precedent. Because the illegal query saved a life, emergency relationship inference was normalized and authorization categories gradually widened. The people maintaining it can sincerely argue that the capability works.

Tenuun sees no clean answer: expose everything and harm vulnerable people and lifesaving capabilities; hide everything and allow the exceptional power to normalize.

## The accident
Maral learns enough of Winter 47's collateral consequences to help relocate a vulnerable woman and child. During an urgent winter drive, the vehicle crashes on an icy road. No villain intentionally causes the crash. A network volunteer dies; Maral suffers physical injury and trauma.

Her memory of the relevant period becomes inaccessible.

Maral's mother knows she used to talk to an unknown person at night and notices the relevant memories cause distress. She chooses not to restore the topic, believing she is protecting her daughter. She repeats Tenuun's moral flaw in another form.

## Tenuun after the accident
Tenuun learns Maral survived and has a memory gap. His first instinct is to decide that forgetting him is better for her. He listens again to her old voice message and recognizes his core flaw.

For the first time, he refuses to decide for her.

## Final plan
Tenuun has enough evidence to expose hidden GRAPH uses, but every direct route sacrifices people:
- full leak exposes vulnerable identities and Maral's illegal query;
- destruction protects individuals but leaves Protocol 12 intact;
- shutdown damages legitimate emergency capability.

He prepares a path that lets Maral reconstruct the truth and choose whether to complete the final relationship edge.

## Disappearance night
- 18:40 — Tenuun confronts Orgil. Orgil asks for the evidence and offers to get him out. Tenuun refuses.
- 20:12 — security architect and university friend Munkh calls for 8m17s. Munkh reported Tenuun to Orgil, believing he was saving his friend from himself.
- 21:17 — Tenuun gives encrypted drive to sister Tuya.
- 22:03 — meets Saruul and activates the final decoy topology.
- 22:41 — asks brother Bilguun to transport and power on a device, creating a later false 'alive' signal.
- 23:08 — Tenuun leaves northern Arvantai in an unregistered old vehicle while security follows the decoy device.

## Tenuun's real status
Tenuun is alive when the game begins. He is hidden in a small wooden safehouse outside the city.

Cruel irony: he has something resembling his dream—wooden house, silence, distance from the city—but no wife, children, garden or Maral. He is living his deepest fear.

## The phone
Tenuun prepares his old phone, selected evidence and encrypted archive. The package is addressed to Maral and contains only the phone and a paper reading `18473`.

He can choose not to send it after her accident. He listens to her voice message and leaves the choice to her.

## Game opening
Eleven days after Tenuun is publicly reported missing, Maral receives the package.

The first question is: Why did a missing stranger send me his phone?

## Final reveal chain
1. Tenuun is more human than his cold recent persona suggests.
2. GRAPH exists and Tenuun did illegal things.
3. `F17`, an unknown woman Tenuun protected, has mounting similarities to Maral.
4. `F17 = Maral`.
5. Maral operated the unauthorized Winter 47 query.
6. Tenuun knew her identity and hid it.
7. Tenuun was not the center of a criminal network; he made himself a decoy center.
8. Tenuun is currently alive.
9. Maral's investigation itself raises GRAPH's confidence in his hidden identity/location.

## Final lock
GRAPH reaches approximately 91% identity/location confidence. One final relationship edge can produce an exact location.

Tenuun leaves a note whose core meaning is:
> You once told me not to decide for you. I did not listen then. I listened this time. If you reached this point, the next choice is not mine.

Choices:
- `TRACE` — complete the relationship edge and reveal Tenuun's location, also exposing him to GRAPH.
- `SEVER` — destroy the final link and preserve his invisibility.

No GOOD/BAD label.

## Canon ending
Maral chooses `SEVER`.

Confidence falls from 91% to zero. Tenuun's location returns to UNKNOWN.

The emotional resolution is not 'she saves her lover.' She respects the autonomy of a person she may never meet again, exactly where Tenuun had repeatedly failed to respect hers.

## Love remains unresolved
Never confirm that Tenuun loved Maral. Evidence may include:
- he kept 18473;
- replayed the call containing her laugh;
- remembered the café;
- added raspberries to cabin plans;
- hid her identity;
- pushed her away;
- finally respected her request.

The player may believe this is love. The game does not certify it.

## Final audio
A recovered old call contains ordinary conversation: Maral laughs, teases Tenuun for acting too grown-up, he quietly hums and denies singing, and they joke about raspberries.

There is no exposition and no confession.

## Final image
Days later, Maral places a small raspberry plant on her balcony. She finally grieves something she cannot fully remember.
