# Character Bible

## Maral Enkhtuvshin — protagonist
- Age: 25
- Education: International Relations
- Work: junior specialist, foreign affairs ministry; multilateral / strategic technology cooperation exposure
- Family: only child; close to mother; emotionally less close to father
- Surface: calm, polite, rational, competent
- Strength: sees competing truths and consequences
- Weakness: can intellectualize pain and delay personal decisions
- Memory state: general identity intact; circumscribed gap around Winter 47 / 18473 / accident period
- Unconscious tells: scratches/thumb-picks under stress; dislikes coriander; particular punctuation habits in text
- Arc: observer → implicated participant → remembered self → person capable of choosing despite uncertainty
- Moral wound: her unauthorized GRAPH query saved one life while exposing others
- Final growth: chooses without outsourcing responsibility and accepts the cost

## Tenuun Batzorig — missing man
- Age: 25
- Work: software engineer, Tenkhleg Data Infrastructure
- Family: poor but loving; parents Oyunaa and Batzorig; sister Tuya; brother Bilguun
- Childhood: funny, social, sings, protective older brother
- Early ambition: startup, wealth, lift family from poverty
- Adult appearance: quiet, restrained, observant, often exhausted
- Hidden remaining self: jokes with siblings, re-emerges strongly in 18473 conversations
- Deep dream: wooden house, spouse, children, dog, greenhouse, fruit/vegetable garden, travel
- Deep fear: surviving while unable to live with the person he loves
- Core flaw: protects people by deciding for them
- Moral contradictions: peace/injustice, protection/autonomy, truth/lies
- Relationship to Maral: first attracted to her reasoning, then laugh, then appearance; never explicit love confirmation
- Symbol: humming/singing returns around Maral

## Orgil Sodnom — CEO / mentor
- Age: early 40s
- Role: founder, Tenkhleg Data Infrastructure
- Relationship to Tenuun: mentor, benefactor, father-like figure
- Truth: encouraged first unauthorized access; knew more about GRAPH than Tenuun
- Moral position: company, jobs, system stability and years of work are real human values
- Lie: minimizes how deliberately he used Tenuun's ambition
- Suspicion role: primary early suspect
- Key line philosophy: 'You think destroying the company hurts only me?'

## Tselmeg — GRAPH state administrator
- Age: late 30s/40s
- Role: public-sector operational/security administrator
- Belief: exceptional capabilities are justified when delay kills people
- Evidence: real successful emergency cases
- Lie: describes exceptional access as rarer and more controlled than it became
- Not a murderer
- Mirror: Maral's Winter 47 reasoning at institutional scale

## Saruul — shelter network operator
- Age: early 30s
- Role: informal network member helping people hide from violent/controlling situations
- Initial relationship to Tenuun: anger; his technology exposed people
- Later relationship: wary collaborator
- Lie: denies meeting Tenuun on disappearance night
- Value protected: vulnerable people's invisibility
- Important: not a saint; the network also makes decisions for people under urgent conditions

## Tuya Batzorig — younger sister
- Age: 21
- Function: first family member to notice Tenuun changed
- Sees: his silence, insomnia, disappearance of singing, stress
- Possesses: encrypted drive
- Lie: says she knows nothing about it
- Knows: half-truth about an unknown woman Tenuun protected
- Does not confirm love

## Bilguun Batzorig — younger brother
- Age: 18
- Function: worships Tenuun as family hero
- Action: moves/powers Tenuun's device after disappearance
- Effect: creates Hope Event #2
- Lie: initially omits his role
- Mirror: player's temptation to excuse Tenuun because of affection

## Munkh — security architect / old friend
- Age: 25–27
- History: university friend; shared coding and startup dreams
- Worldview: engineers build systems; policy use is not fully theirs to control
- Betrayal: reported Tenuun's secret activity to Orgil
- Motive: stop Tenuun from destroying his life and going to prison
- Key moral reversal: accuses Tenuun of resenting others for deciding for him while he constantly decides for everyone else

## Oyunaa — Tenuun's mother
- Warm, practical, proud
- Humanizes Tenuun through mundane family history
- Does not know full story

## Batzorig — Tenuun's father
- Loving, practical, less verbally expressive
- Represents care through actions

## Maral's mother
- Knows fragments of daughter's pre-accident anonymous contact
- Chooses silence after accident to protect Maral
- Repeats project's core moral mistake through parental love

## The 17-year-old girl — Winter 47 subject
- Daughter of a high-level foreign visitor
- Fled a controlling/abusive environment voluntarily
- Required urgent medical help
- Maral's illegal query genuinely saved her life
- Never reduce her to a plot device; Phase 04 content must give her agency and credible reasons
