# Phase 05 — Audio & Emotional Polish

## Goal
Make timing, silence, sound and visual pacing support—not replace—the writing.

## Work
- real voice assets or approved temporary performances;
- transcripts;
- phone compression;
- sparse music;
- winter/city ambience;
- reveal pauses;
- final ordinary-call mix;
- raspberry ending scene.

## Rule
Never use swelling music to force emotion where the scene does not earn it.
