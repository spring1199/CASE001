# Audio / Visual Direction

## Mood
Cold city, ordinary interiors, realistic phone acoustics, silence used as tension.

## Music
Sparse. Avoid continuous thriller score.
- opening: almost none;
- discoveries: minimal tonal texture;
- 18473 intimacy: warmer but restrained;
- reveals: prefer silence and diegetic audio;
- final choice: little/no music until after selection;
- final ordinary call should carry emotional weight without swelling orchestration.

## Tenuun motif
Singing/humming.
- childhood media: openly sings;
- adult work life: absent;
- around Maral: quiet humming returns;
- final recovered call uses this motif.

## Maral motif
Laugh. Do not overuse. One important early voice recording and one final return are enough.

## City sound
- apartment corridor reverb;
- elevator chime;
- winter wind;
- traffic;
- convenience-store ambience;
- car heater fan;
- phone compression;
- distant city hum.

## Images
Photos should look mundane and imperfect: family snapshots, screenshots, parking photos, work whiteboards, accidental compositions. Critical clues should survive reasonable compression and have accessible inspect alternatives.

## Violence
18+ realism is allowed, but do not use graphic gore as primary emotional mechanism. The story's pressure comes from consequence, privacy, coercion and grief.
