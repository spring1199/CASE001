# Case #001 Content Authoring Guide

## Dialogue law
People do not speak to explain the plot. Every message should have a local reason to exist.

## Tenuun voice
Recent Tenuun:
- short replies;
- precise language;
- dry humor rarely escapes;
- avoids emotional declarations;
- lies by omission more often than flamboyant fabrication.

Younger/family Tenuun:
- teasing;
- quick jokes;
- voice notes;
- sings/hums;
- affectionate through actions.

18473 Tenuun:
- his older humor gradually returns;
- asks questions he would not ask anyone at work;
- never becomes an overwritten poet.

## Maral voice
- sees two sides;
- uses light teasing rather than flirtation-first dialogue;
- emotionally intelligent without sounding like a therapist;
- final pre-accident voice message is angry but not theatrical.

## Romance rule
Do not tell the player to fall in love with Tenuun. The player should voluntarily open the next recording because they want to hear him.

## Mundane artifacts
At least 60% of phone content should feel like ordinary life, but optional background should remain concise enough not to become spam.

Use:
- family shopping messages;
- bills;
- delivery notifications;
- memes;
- work reminders;
- sibling jokes;
- startup notes;
- grocery searches;
- minor arguments.

## Required emotional motifs
- laughter;
- singing/humming;
- raspberry;
- wooden house;
- 'do not decide for me';
- Tenuun's shift from ambition to peace.

## Mature content
18+ themes may include abuse, coercive control, alcohol, sexuality, profanity and violence where realistic. Do not use them merely for shock.

## Mongolian texture
Dialogue should sound like contemporary Mongolian adults, with natural code-switching only where profession/social context supports it. Avoid turning every character into slang caricature.
